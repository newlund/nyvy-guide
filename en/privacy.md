# Privacy

<p class="lead">In short: everything Nyvo stores stays on your TV. There is no Nyvo server, no account with us and no statistics about how you use the app.</p>

## What is stored, on your TV

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your account details (server, username and password, or the playlist address) | To sign in with your provider. They are stored **encrypted** with a key that is created on the TV and can't be read out. |
| The catalogue and the TV Guide | So lists and the guide show quickly without downloading them every time |
| Favorites, resume points and your settings | So the app remembers your choices |
| The parental control PIN and locked categories | To protect content |

**Sign out** under *Settings → Account* removes the account and all stored data from the TV.

## What is sent, and where

- **To your provider:** signing in, downloading the catalogue and TV Guide, and the streams you watch. The provider handles these under its own terms.
- **To addresses you enter yourself** (playlist and TV Guide).
- **Nothing to Nyvo** or anyone else: no statistics, no ads, no tracking.

## This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies or analytics. GitHub may log visits under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Content

Nyvo contains no TV channels, films or series, and sells no subscriptions. Everything you watch comes from your own provider.
