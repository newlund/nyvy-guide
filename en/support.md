# Support Nyvy

<p class="lead">Nyvy is free and has no ads. If you like the app and want to help it keep improving, you can buy me a coffee. Thank you!</p>

<a class="button" href="https://buymeacoffee.com/nyvy">☕ Buy Me a Coffee</a>

A contribution is voluntary and unlocks nothing extra. Nyvy works the same for everyone.

## Other ways to help

- **Report problems and ideas** on [GitHub](https://github.com/newlund/nyvy-guide/issues) (you need a free account): what you did, what happened and which version you have (*Settings* → *About*). The page is public, so leave out your provider's address and login.
- **Tell others** who have an LG TV and an IPTV subscription.
